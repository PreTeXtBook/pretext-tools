Math

  • $x^2$ becomes <m>x^2</m>.
  • $$\sum_i i$$ becomes <md>…</md>.

Escaping

  • a < b becomes a &lt; b; likewise > and &.
  • Inside math, a < b becomes a \lt b.

Paragraphs

  • Inside a <p>, press Enter twice, or Shift+Enter, to start the next paragraph.
  • Elsewhere, Shift+Enter starts a new <p>.

Environments

  • On a blank line outside a paragraph, type theorem: (or definition:, proof:, …) and press Enter.

Notes

  • Every shortcut is one undo step: Ctrl+Z restores what you typed.
  • All logic comes from @pretextbook/typing-shortcuts; this page only calls registerMonacoTypingShortcuts.
