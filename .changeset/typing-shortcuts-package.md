New package: editor-agnostic PreTeXt typing shortcuts (`$x$` → `<m>x</m>`, `$$x$$` → `<md>x</md>`, escaping a bare `<`/`>`/`&` as `&lt;`/`&gt;`/`&amp;` or `\lt`/`\gt` in math, double Enter or Shift+Enter to start a new paragraph, and `theorem:` + Enter to insert an environment snippet), with a Monaco adapter (`registerMonacoTypingShortcuts`).
